# 🌺 Lei Catcher — Sunset Luau

A single-file HTML5 canvas arcade game set at a beachfront luau at sunset —
tiki hut, tiki torches, palm trees, and a sky full of falling orchid blossoms.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no dependencies.

- **Move:** ← / → arrow keys or A / D — on mobile, touch and drag
- **Catch** falling orchid blossoms in your basket to string leis (8 blossoms = 1 lei)
- **Golden hibiscus** is worth +50 and counts as two blossoms
- **Pineapples** are a +30 bonus
- **Dodge coconuts!** Three bonks and the luau is over
- Chain catches without missing to build a **combo multiplier**
- Every completed lei levels you up (faster drops, bigger bonuses) — every third lei restores a life

Best score is saved locally in your browser.
