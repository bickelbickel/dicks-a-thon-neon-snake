# 🌋 Aloha Raiders — Pele's Fury

A Raiden-style vertical-scrolling shoot-'em-up set over the Hawaiian islands,
starring the two pilots from the photo (see the title-screen polaroid 📸).
Single HTML file + one photo, no build, no dependencies — open `index.html`
in any browser.

## The mission

Fly a koa-wood **outrigger gunship** north over the Pacific. Fight through
coconut drone squadrons, tiki-mask fleets, lava sprites, war canoes, and
pineapple AA turrets dug in on the islands — then face **Pele's Volcano**,
a multi-phase boss that aims fireball spreads, lobs arcing lava bombs that
burst into fragments, and unleashes twin bullet spirals as it gets angry.
Beat it and the squadron pushes on to the next, harder island chain.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrows / WASD | drag anywhere |
| Focus (slow, shows hitbox) | hold Shift | — |
| Fire | automatic | automatic |
| Tsunami bomb | B or X | tap the blue button |
| Music on/off | M | — |

## Raiden-style systems

- **Power-ups** dropped by golden carrier drones: `P` weapon level (5 levels,
  from twin bolts to a fan of 7 + homing missiles), `B` extra tsunami bomb,
  `L` extra life
- **Tsunami bomb** clears every bullet on screen and slams everything for
  heavy damage
- Small hitbox, mercy invulnerability, and a bullet-clear on respawn
- Hi-score saved in your browser

## Extras

- Synthesized **Hawaiian slack-key / ukulele soundtrack** (WebAudio — uke
  strum vamp, bass, and a sliding steel-guitar melody), plus all sound effects
- Parallax ocean with waves, sun glints, drifting clouds, palm-dotted islands
  and smoking minor volcanoes
- Jump straight to the boss for testing: open `index.html#boss`
